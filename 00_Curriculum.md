Status: scaffold; module names are provisional until approved by the course author.

## Workload

- Theoretical instruction: 16 academic hours = 8 modules.
- Laboratories: 16 academic hours = 8 sessions.
- Self-study: 88 academic hours.
- Total: 120 academic hours, 4 ECTS.
- Final assessment: pass/fail credit.

## Design principle

Each module pairs source-based theory material with one laboratory. The theory establishes the knowledge and decision framework required for practice; the laboratory produces and verifies an artifact in the cumulative student project.
